Before you go

Useful travel information

The basics you need to know before planning your Greek island holiday.

Best time to visit

May–June and September for fewer crowds; July–August for the full summer atmosphere.

Currency

The euro (€) is used everywhere. Cards are widely accepted, but it helps to carry cash on smaller islands.

Getting around

Ferries for island hopping, domestic flights for longer distances, and a car for exploring each island.

Language

Greek is the official language, but English is widely spoken in hotels and tourist areas.
