Frequently asked questions

Before you book your trip

What's the best time for island hopping?

May, June and September offer warm weather with fewer crowds and calmer ferry schedules. July and August are busier but come with the full summer atmosphere.

How many islands can I visit in 10 days?

We usually recommend 3 to 4 islands for 10 days, so you have enough time in each destination without spending most of your trip travelling.

Do I need a car on every island?

It depends on the island's size. On larger islands like Crete or Rhodes it helps a lot, while on smaller islands buses or bicycles are often enough.

How do I book ferry tickets?

You can compare routes and companies through our guide and book directly online, usually a few weeks ahead during the summer months.
